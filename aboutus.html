About the Office

Who We Are

The Municipal Planning and Development Coordinator (MPDC) is the key technical arm of the local government in planning and development.

Mandate & Functions

The MPDC is responsible for the formulation of the Comprehensive Development Plan (CDP), Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP), and the Local Development Investment Program (LDIP). We coordinate with all departments to ensure that development projects are aligned with the municipality’s vision.

  • Formulate and update the CLUP and CDP
  • Coordinate the preparation of the Annual Investment Program
  • Conduct socioeconomic research and data analysis
  • Monitor and evaluate development projects
  • Provide technical assistance to barangays
